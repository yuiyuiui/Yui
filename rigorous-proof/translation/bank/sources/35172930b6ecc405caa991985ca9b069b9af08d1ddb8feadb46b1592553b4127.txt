The constants are frozen in the following order.  Shrink the small-alpha
constant if necessary so that `0<alpha_0<=1`.  Let `C_L` be the absolute
threshold in the published large-radius theorem and set

\[
A=(C_L+1)^2.
\tag{3.3}
\]

Apply the growing-alpha theorem with this fixed `A`; it supplies a fixed
threshold `alpha_1=alpha_1(A)>=1`.  Finally set

\[
a=\frac{\alpha_0}{2},\qquad b=2\alpha_1.
\tag{3.4}
\]

Then (3.2) covers every layer between the small and growing regimes,
including rounding at their endpoints.  The order matters because the
threshold `alpha_1` in Theorem 2.1 may depend on `A`.
