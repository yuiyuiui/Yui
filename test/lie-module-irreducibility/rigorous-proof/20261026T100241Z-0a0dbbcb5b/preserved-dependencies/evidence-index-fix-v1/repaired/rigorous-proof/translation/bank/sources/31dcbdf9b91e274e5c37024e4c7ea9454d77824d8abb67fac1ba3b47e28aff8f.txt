The finite-product extension and uniform-on-circle limiting argument are now
compiled.  The declarations
`tendstoUniformlyOn_analyticThetaProduct`,
`tendsto_circleIntegral_analyticThetaProduct_div_pow`, and
`circleIntegral_analyticThetaProduct_div_pow` prove the exact statement

\[
 [z^n]\prod_{i=1}^d\Theta_i(z)
 = \frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint_{|z|=\rho}
 \frac{\prod_{i=1}^d\Theta_i(z)}{z^{n+1}}\,dz,

\]

with the coefficient identified with the already formalized bilateral theta
coefficient.  The proof passes through the finite partial products and the
explicit uniform limit; it does not introduce an abstract analytic-function
axiom.
