It remains to make the local compactness uniform on the unit ball.  Since
\(D\widetilde{\mathcal P}_\chi q=q\), equation (4.9) gives the exact
cancellation

\[
 \tag{5.2}
 D\mathcal R_{t,\ell}^{\varepsilon}q
 =\left(\frac{x}{2t}+\lambda
       -\frac{i\varepsilon}{2t}\right)
   \mathcal V_{t,\ell}^{\varepsilon}q
 +\frac{I_+(\mathcal V_{t,\ell}^{\varepsilon}q)}{4\pi i t}.
\]

The chirp estimate (2.2) gives

\[
 \frac{|I_+(\mathcal Vq)|}{t}
 \le C_{\sigma,R}t^{-1/2}\|q\|_{\mathcal Y_\sigma}.
\]

On a fixed compact set \(K\), (0.26), (1.16), and (4.3a) imply

\[
 \|\mathcal Vq\|_{L^\infty(K)}
 \le C_{K,R}(1+\log t)\|q\|_{\mathcal Y_\sigma}.
\]
