## 2. Reduction to integer squared radii

For every real `t>=0`,

\[
tB_2^d\cap\mathbb Z^d
=\{y\in\mathbb Z^d:|y|^2\le\lfloor t^2\rfloor\}.
\tag{2.1}
\]

Thus, with `M_(d,n)` denoting the normalized average over
`B_(d,n)={y:|y|^2<=n}`,

\[
\sup_{t\ge0}|\mathcal M_tf|
=\sup_{n\in\mathbb N_0}|M_{d,n}f|.
\tag{2.2}
\]

No radius discretization error is introduced.
