**Internal normalization bridge.**  Put \(a=2\pi\).  For an
\(L^2\) class \(f\), define the transform in the convention (0.1) by

\[
 \widehat f(\eta):=(\mathcal F_{\rm ml}f)(\eta/a),
 \tag{M6.1}
\]

and for \(H\in L^2(\mathbb R)\) define

\[
 \mathcal F^{-1}H
 :=\mathcal F_{\rm ml}^{-1}\bigl[\omega\mapsto H(a\omega)\bigr].
 \tag{M6.2}
\]

We first prove, rather than assume, compatibility with the integral
transform.  Let \(f\in L^1\cap L^2\), let

\[
 H_1(\omega)=\int_{\mathbb R}e^{-2\pi ix\omega}f(x)\,dx,
 \qquad H_2=\mathcal F_{\rm ml}f\in L^2.
\]
