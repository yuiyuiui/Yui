For each such x the function of r is constant on J, so u_t(x,r)=0 there; likewise its spatial derivatives vanish on the open set R^n\\C_J. By smoothness and continuity of the derivatives, those derivatives also vanish on the boundary of C_J. Thus for every r∈J the functions u_t(·,r), u_{x_j}(·,r), and e(·,r) vanish outside C_J. The function e(·,r) is continuous and bounded on the compact set C_J, hence measurable and Lebesgue integrable on R^n (it vanishes off a compact set).
